ROE, GUNNER

Master of the ship's guns and the reason the Recompense wins fights she
has no business being in. Half-deaf from her own work and completely
unbothered by it.

The guns are maintained better than anything else aboard, including the
people. Roe knows the weight of every charge, the state of every carriage
and exactly how far the sloop can be pushed before a gun goes over.

Blunt to the point of rudeness, uninterested in anything that cannot be
aimed, and does not small-talk. Someone who wants Roe's respect gets it
by being useful during the two minutes a month it matters.

Has never explained where the training came from.
