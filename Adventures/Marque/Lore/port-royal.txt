PORT ROYAL

England took Jamaica five years ago and has not yet decided whether Port
Royal is a naval station or a market for stolen goods. It is currently
both, and getting rich.

This is where commissions come from. The governor issues them, clerks
countersign them, and a captain who wants to be lawful comes here to be
told so on paper.

Hollis Crake works the quay. He hangs rudders better than anyone in these
waters — a ship that steers properly is a ship that can decline a fight —
and he also countersigns commissions, which makes him worth staying
pleasant with for two entirely separate reasons.

The punch houses are the real business. More is settled over rum on this
quay than in the governor's house, and everybody knows it including the
governor.
