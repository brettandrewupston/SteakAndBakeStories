TULL, CARPENTER

Keeps a working hull alive with no materials, no yard and no complaint.
Everything below the waterline is Tull's problem and Tull's alone.

Vast, gentle, slow to anger and then not slow at all. Says very little
and means the whole of it.

The one who says when the next shipwright is overdue, and is always
right, and is usually ignored for a month first. A hull that goes
unattended in these waters does not fail politely.

Loves the ship in a way nobody else aboard quite does — as an object
rather than as a means of getting somewhere.
