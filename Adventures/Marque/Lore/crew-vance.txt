VANCE, QUARTERMASTER

Divides every share, hears every dispute, and would have the ship if the
captain went over the side. Second aboard by the plain fact of being the
only other person the crew would follow.

Exact. Unhurried. Impossible to rush into an opinion, and says the
difficult thing once, plainly, and then lets it sit there rather than
arguing it.

Knows the commission is false, has known since the first day, and has
never said so in any port. That is not loyalty exactly and neither of
them has ever called it that. It is simply true, permanently, and it sits
between them in every conversation about where the ship goes next.

Could end the captain in an afternoon by walking into the right office.
Has not.
