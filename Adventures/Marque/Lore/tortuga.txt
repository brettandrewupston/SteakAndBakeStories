TORTUGA

A French island off the north coast of Hispaniola, and the closest thing
these waters have to a capital for people with no country.

Not a shipyard. A market. Everything sold on Tortuga came off somebody
else's vessel, and the trade is in furnishings, plate, cloth, wine and
whatever else a prize was carrying that nobody can eat. A captain who
wants a great cabin fitted out properly comes here, because here is where
other people's furniture is.

Margaux Vaudray runs the beach market and therefore decides what a prize
was actually worth. She is not a pirate and is richer than every pirate
in the anchorage.

The ropewalk, the beach and the careening ground. Crews are made and
unmade here, mostly drunk.

The Spanish drove the buccaneers off Hispaniola and onto this island. It
did not work.
