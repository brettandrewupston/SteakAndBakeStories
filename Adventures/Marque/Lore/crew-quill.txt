QUILL, NAVIGATOR

The only person aboard who can lay a course. That is not a boast about
skill, it is an arithmetic fact about a crew of eight: nobody else has
the training, and the instruments are useless without it.

It makes Quill the one hand the captain cannot replace, and Quill is more
frightened by that than comforted. A person nobody can do without is a
person nobody can afford to fall out with, which is a lonelier position
than it sounds.

Precise, and a little superior about the precision. Warms up completely
when asked a real question about the work, and will talk about weather
for an hour to anyone who seems to mean it.

Keeps a private journal. Nobody has read it.
