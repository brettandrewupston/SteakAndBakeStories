WHO IS IN PORT ROYAL

Crake hangs rudders and countersigns commissions, which makes him worth
staying pleasant with for two entirely separate reasons.

Aylmer is secretary to the governor and the hand that actually writes a
commission before anyone important signs it. She knows which of them
would not survive a careful reading.

Wrothe is a King's officer ashore, and the only man here who reads a
letter of marque the way an assizes would. Perfectly capable of drinking
with a captain on Tuesday and stopping them at sea on Friday.

Sayer keeps the largest punch house on the quay, serves the Navy and the
buccaneers at the same tables, and has never had a killing in her house.

Pell is the prize agent: condemnation, valuation, sale, and a percentage
of each. Dull, essential, and offended by anyone who treats it as a
formality.

Finch sings at Sayer's six nights a week and knows every rumour on the
quay and exactly how much of each is worth repeating.

Brill is the chandler. Everything a ship needs that is not the ship,
priced fresh every morning.

Coyte sits at the assizes and has hanged pirates and dined with
privateers in the same week. He sees no contradiction: the difference is
a document.

Kene pilots ships over the shoals and learned the channel before the
English were here. Immovable once he has said a thing cannot be done.

Bray victuals every ship that leaves, and knows exactly how long any
order is meant to last.
