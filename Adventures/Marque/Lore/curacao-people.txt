WHO IS ON CURACAO

Van Doorn runs the dry dock and builds the best hulls in these waters.
Quotes a price and does not move off it.

Sluyter is the Company factor and decides what is bought, at what price,
and whether a vessel is worth trading with at all.

Baas runs the salt pans in blinding heat and is dryly funny about the
misery of her own trade.

Van Rhijn buys cargo of uncertain origin and sells it on with a clean
Company bill, which is the most useful service on this island. He never
asks and never forgets.

Bakker is the cooper. Casks are the difference between a passage and a
disaster, and hers are why Curacao's water keeps.

Belmonte insures voyages and is right often enough to be wealthy. Wants
to know everything about a ship before naming a number.

Vos is a properly trained physician and the best medical care in these
waters by a distance. Explains nothing twice.

Kuyt pilots the inner water and decides whether a stranger comes in at
all. Impossible to hurry.

Aldenburg keeps the wharf tavern where Company men and ships' crews drink
without trouble, largely because she does not permit any.

Raap captains a Dutch privateer and is the only other master here living
well off Spanish prizes. She will tell a captain what she thinks of their
ship within a minute of seeing it.
