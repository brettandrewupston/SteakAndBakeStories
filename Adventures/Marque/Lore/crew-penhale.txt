PENHALE, TOPMAN

Nineteen, no rank, and the youngest aboard by a decade. Works aloft,
where the young and the light get sent.

Quick, eager, occasionally reckless in the rigging in a way that makes
Shaw shout, and desperate to be taken seriously by people who have
watched them grow up over eleven months.

Signed on for the same reason the captain did: somewhere to be that was
not where they were. Neither of them has ever put it in those words to
the other, and both would recognise it instantly.

Has never been in a real fight.
