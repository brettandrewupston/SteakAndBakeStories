CURACAO

Dutch, orderly, and interested in cargo rather than territory. The
warehouses are in straight lines and the ledgers balance.

The Dutch build the cheapest sound hull in the world, which is the whole
reason they carry everybody's freight, and the dry dock here is the
southern end of that advantage. A ship that has been through it takes the
sea better and meets less of it.

Griet van Doorn runs the yard. Brisk, precise, allergic to flattery, and
visibly happier discussing timber than money. She quotes a price and does
not move off it.

The salt pans behind the town are the island's other trade, and the
reason it was worth taking at all.

Curacao sits far south, off the Main. It is the longest passage from
anywhere and the crossing is the price of the hull.
