WHO IS IN SANTO DOMINGO

Nieves cuts the best sail in the Indies and knows what sort of captain
walks into a Spanish port to buy it. He has never mentioned it to a
soldier.

Salcedo reads a stranger's papers at the customs house. He knows half of
what crosses his desk is a lie; the question is whether it is one he is
obliged to notice.

Valdivia keeps the lodging house above the wharf and is the first person
here to notice a guest is not what they claim.

Arce is a canon of the cathedral. Says mass, hears confession, has heard
a great deal, and is genuinely difficult to shock.

Herrera is sergeant of the garrison and decides who walks up into the
city. Sharper than the post requires and bored.

Cabral sells fruit in the square and knows the movements of every ship,
official and soldier here, because they all pass her twice a day.

Ocampo is the apothecary the city goes to before it goes to a physician.
Corrects a doctor without raising her voice.

Balbuena trades quietly with the enemy, like many here, because the
Crown's ships come twice a year and people eat every day.

Catalina Nieves cuts sail beside her father and is already better at the
fine work. Impatient at being introduced as somebody's daughter.

Serna works a boat on the Ozama and knows which officials are on which
gate at which hour. Talks constantly and says nothing.
