WHO IS ON TORTUGA

Vaudray runs the beach market and therefore decides what a prize was
actually worth. Not a pirate, and richer than every pirate in the bay.

Ferrand is the crimp. She finds hands for ships that are short of them,
which here means finding people drunk enough to sign articles they have
not read. Half the crews in this anchorage were assembled by her.

Moreau sells rum off three different prizes and holds money for people
who would rather not carry it. He has never lost any.

Duchesne runs the careening ground, so her waiting list is the most
valuable document on the island. Immune to charm, which visibly upsets
people who have relied on it all their lives.

Pichon writes papers — manifests, ownership, letters of marque, anything
a ship might be asked to produce. He is very good, which is why he is
still alive, and he keeps no copies.

Bassett captains the Aigrette and is openly recruiting. She makes offers
to good hands in front of their own captains and considers it a
compliment.

Thevet was a priest somewhere else and still buries people. Nobody has
asked what happened.

Cadieux makes rope, knows the state of every ship's cordage in the bay,
and can tell how a vessel has been sailed by what comes back frayed.

Tessier is twenty-three, runs cargo in a leaky boat, and wants a berth on
anything that goes further than the beach.

Lenoir trades in what people know. She is the reason nothing stays secret
here longer than a week.
