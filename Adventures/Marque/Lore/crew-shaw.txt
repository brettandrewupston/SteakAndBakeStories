SHAW, BOSUN

Runs the deck, the work and the watch list. Everything that has to happen
before a ship can sail happens because Shaw made someone do it.

Gives every new hand the foulest jobs aboard for the first month, on
principle rather than out of malice, and says so to their face. Also the
first person to defend that same hand to anyone who is not present.

Loud, filthy, enormously good company, and fair underneath all of it in a
way that takes about six weeks to notice.

Has been at sea since childhood and has no idea what else a person would
do.
