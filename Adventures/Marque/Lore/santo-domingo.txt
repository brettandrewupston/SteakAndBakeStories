SANTO DOMINGO

The oldest European city in the Americas, Spanish, walled, and on the
same island as Tortuga — which means it is a short hop from the one place
in these waters that exists to prey on it.

Spanish rig is built to cross an ocean rather than potter between
islands, and the rigging lofts on the Ozama cut the best canvas in the
Indies. A suit of it shortens every passage a ship makes afterwards.

Rodrigo Nieves is master of those lofts. Courteous to the point of being
unreadable, slow, and excellent company once business is settled. He
knows exactly what sort of captain walks into a Spanish port to buy sail,
and he has never yet mentioned it to a soldier.

It is the closest port and the most dangerous one. A privateer preying on
Spanish shipping who walks through a Spanish customs house is relying
entirely on a piece of paper and their own nerve, and one of those is
false.
