THE RECOMPENSE

A sloop. One mast, fore-and-aft rigged, shallow enough in the draught to
run into water no King's ship can follow her into and fast enough on a
reach to decline any fight she does not want.

She carries eight hands and the captain. That is a small company by the
standards of these waters and it is deliberate: a sloop this size cannot
feed more, and every share taken is a share divided.

She is not a warship and never was. She is a working vessel with guns on
her because everything out here has guns on it.

Below: a great cabin in the stern with windows onto the wake, a berth
deck where the hammocks touch, a galley the cook rules absolutely, and a
hold with the water casks, the stores and the dark. On deck, the waist
where the whole company ends up, and the tops, which is the only place
aboard two people can be genuinely alone and everyone below knows it.

Her name is a joke that stopped being funny. Recompense is what a letter
of marque authorises a captain to take.
