BONNET, COOK

Feeds everybody and reads everybody. The galley is the ship's real
parliament: everything gets decided there and merely announced elsewhere.

Warm, nosy, and armed with the entire company's business. Trades
information in food, which on a small ship is the strongest currency
there is.

The person to ask what the crew actually think before spending anything
finding out. Will tell the captain the truth, once, quietly, and not
repeat it.

Has sailed with more captains than anyone else aboard and has buried
several.
