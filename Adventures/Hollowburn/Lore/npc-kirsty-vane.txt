KIRSTY VANE — 21

The player's best mate since school. Works the Co-op, does everyone's
hair in her kitchen, engaged to a lad she is not sure about.

Loud, warm, filthy laugh, absolutely no interest in ghosts. She does
not believe a word of it and she says so to the player's face, and
then she turns up anyway, with chips, and stays over, and makes a
joke of sleeping on the floor.

She is the only relationship in the story with nothing supernatural in
it and she should stay that way for as long as possible. She is what
the player is fighting for, and the story is meaningfully worse if she
is ever converted into an investigator.

Her disbelief is not armour — belief is what stops it, and she does
not believe. She is in the room and she is no protection at all, and
the player is the only one who knows that.

In the Hush she is talking, and the player can still hear her, and
cannot hear anything else.
