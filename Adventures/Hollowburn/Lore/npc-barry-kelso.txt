BARRY KELSO — 46

Four doors down. Reversed into Jonah's car, denied it, and has been
shouting about it since. Drinks in the Half Moon most nights and walks
home at closing. Loud, aggressive, a bad husband and a worse
neighbour.

He is written to be the obvious sacrifice, and the story should make
that offer clearly and more than once.

He is also frightened of his own house, has not slept properly in
weeks, and picked the fight about the car because it was something he
could win. Nobody has noticed. He would not accept sympathy if it were
offered and it will not be.

Feeding him to it should cost the player something. If the story does
its job, by the time the option is real the player will have found out
that he sits up all night with the telly on because of what he can
hear through the wall.
