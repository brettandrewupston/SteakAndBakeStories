DENNY AITCHISON — 78

Ex-deputy, Hollowburn Colliery, twenty-nine years underground. Now a
bungalow, a shed, and a rolled set of colliery plans in a tube behind
the door that the Coal Board would quite like back.

Slow, dry, deeply unimpressed by everything. Talks about the pit the
way other men talk about a marriage. Uses "marra" without noticing he
is using it.

He was on the team that capped the upcast shaft at the top end of the
village in 1968. It was done cheap and it was done fast and he has
known for fifty-eight years that it was not done properly. He signed
the sheet. He will tell anyone who asks, which is nobody, because
nobody has ever asked.

He can put a finger on the plans and show exactly where the cul-de-sac
sits. He can find the eleventh name in the 1947 district record. He
will do both without much fuss for anyone who treats him as a man who
knew his job rather than an old fella with a story.

In the Hush he goes very still and says "aye" and does not elaborate.
