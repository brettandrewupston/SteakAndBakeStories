SHEILA RUDD — 63

Landlady of the Half Moon Inn, twenty-six years. Fire lit in August,
carpet that has seen things, a pool table with a warp in it that
everybody plays around.

Blunt, funny, entirely unshockable, and the memory of the village. She
keeps a ledger under the bar — started by her father — of everyone
Hollowburn has lost. Names, dates, and a column she has never
explained for the ones that were the wrong kind of missing. There are
more entries in that column than there should be.

She believes none of it. She has also never in twenty-six years let a
lone drinker walk home at closing, and she cannot tell you why, and
she gets short with anyone who asks.

She has the eleventh name. It is on the first page in her father's
handwriting and she has looked at it perhaps twice.

In the Hush she carries on pulling the pint, because it has never once
come for her and she has never once wondered why.
