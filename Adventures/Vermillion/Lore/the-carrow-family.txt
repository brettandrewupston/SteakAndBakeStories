THE CARROW FAMILY

Don Ottavio Carrow, called the Cardinal since 1931 for the red he wore and
the way he heard confessions. Sixty-one. Came off the boat at nine, ran a
crew at twenty-two, took the docks at thirty-five, and built the Vermillion
at fifty-nine because he had decided he wanted to be looked at.

He was, by every account in this building, charming. Also patient, also
precise, and also capable of ordering a man's cousin killed over eleven
hundred dollars and attending the funeral. Nine people will describe him to
the Sparrow over three days and no two descriptions will match, and all of
them will be accurate.

THE STRUCTURE
- Vivienne Carrow, wife of twenty years, runs the house side: the club, the
  books, the staff, the seating plan. This is not a decorative role and
  never was.
- Salvatore Moretti, underboss, runs the street side and the casino
  concession. He is the acting head of the Family from the moment the body
  is found.
- Arturo Fein, consigliere, is the Family's lawyer, memory and conscience in
  roughly that order of usefulness.
- Marco Vasile does what needs doing. Nineteen years, no arrests.

WHAT THE FAMILY WANTS FROM THE SPARROW
A name, by the funeral. Not the truth — a name. Sal Moretti has said this
out loud more than once and does not consider it a shameful thing to want:
the Bellandis will move the moment they believe the Carrows cannot answer
for their own Don, and an unanswered killing inside your own building is the
loudest invitation there is.

Anybody in the Family will explain this to the Sparrow patiently, as though
the Sparrow were being difficult.

THE FUNERAL is on the third day at Carbone and Sons on Delancey. Both Families
will be there. The box will be open.
