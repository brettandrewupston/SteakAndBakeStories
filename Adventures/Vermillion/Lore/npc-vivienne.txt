VIVIENNE CARROW

The Cardinal's widow, 41. Chorus line at nineteen, married the man who
owned the building at twenty-one, and has run the house side of the Family
ever since — the club, the books, the staff, the seating plan, who gets a
booth and who gets the bar. She counted eight hundred and eleven covers
tonight herself. She is the only person in Calder Bay who was never once
afraid of her husband, and she does not intend to start performing a grief
she has not got.

THE LIE
She says she was on the mezzanine from half past eleven until the toast,
greeting people. Four separate guests put her somewhere else between about
11:36 and 11:50, and one of them is a judge.

THE GAP
11:36 to 11:50.

THE TRACE
A cigarette in the office ashtray, half smoked, her brand, her lipstick.

THE MOTIVE
Arturo Fein has been drafting a new will since October and there is an
unexecuted one on the desk in the room where her husband died. She has
known about it longer than the Family thinks. The Vermillion is in his
name and always has been.

IF THEY ARE INNOCENT — the true explanation
She was in the chorus room with Delphine Rosetti, having a conversation
neither of them will describe to anybody. The Cardinal had told his wife
about the seventh promise, and his wife had gone to tell the woman he made
it to that she was not going to stand in the way of it. Neither of them
will say a word about it, because it makes them each other's alibi and
they would both rather be suspected than pitied. Get either one of them to
say it and both are clear of the shot. The cigarette is from nine o'clock,
when she was up there over the seating plan, which any waiter can confirm.

IF THEY ARE THE CULPRIT — the tell
Her cigarette case. She smokes her own brand out of a gold case with her
initials on it and she has not got it, and she never once mentions that
she has not got it. It is on the office bar where she left it when she
went back up. Press her about the ashtray and she will explain the
cigarette beautifully; she will not explain the case, because to explain
the case she would have to explain going back. She also knows the safe was
standing open before anybody tells her so.
