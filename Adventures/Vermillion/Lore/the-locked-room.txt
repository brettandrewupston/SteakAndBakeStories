THE LOCKED ROOM

The office door was bolted on the inside and the Cardinal threw that bolt
himself every night of his life, which means the puzzle is not how the
killer got in — he let them in, he poured them a drink — but how they
got out.

There are three ways out of that room. All three are real, all three are
discoverable, and in any given telling of this story exactly ONE of them was
used and shows real recent use. The other two are still there and still
findable, which is what makes eliminating them work as detection instead of
as a formality.

1. THE SERVICE DUMBWAITER
   Galley to the office pantry, six floors, hand-cranked from either end. Put
   in so the Cardinal could eat without coming down. The car is
   twenty-two inches square. A slight person fits, folded, and arrives
   filthy — the shaft has not been cleaned since it was built, and
   anybody who rode it is wearing the shaft on their clothes whether they
   know it or not. The galley end is in plain view of six kitchen staff, so
   using it means the crank was worked from the office end and the car sent
   back up empty afterwards, or that somebody in the galley is lying.

2. THE OLD FREIGHT STAIR
   Runs the full height of the building behind the count-room racks. Welded
   shut in 1946 when the count room went in. The weld was cut in the summer
   of 1948 and a plate hung back over it, and the door at the sixth-floor
   end takes a key of which exactly one exists. The Cardinal had it cut so
   he could reach his own count room without walking through his own casino.
   Fewer people know about it than the Family would like: the Cardinal, the
   man who cut it, Vasile, and anyone who has ever watched the count-room
   clerks jump at a noise behind the racks.

3. THE POWDER-ROOM DOOR
   The sixth-floor landing has a powder room off it, and the powder room
   shares a wall with the office. There is a door in that wall. On the
   powder-room side it is a door. On the office side it was papered over
   when the room was decorated in 1947 and it opens inward if you press the
   panel at hip height, hard, in the right place. Vivienne chose the
   wallpaper. The decorator is in Florida.

WHICH ONE MATTERS
Whichever route was used is the one carrying evidence: dust and grease from
the shaft, a key that should not exist in a pocket, torn wallpaper and paint
under a fingernail. The other two are clean, and proving them clean is half
the work.

The route also narrows the field, because not everybody can use every route.
The dumbwaiter takes a slight person only. The stair takes the one key. The
powder-room door takes somebody who knows the panel is there — which is a
much shorter list than the Family believes, and is not the list the Family
would guess.
