WENDY ODELL, 47 — Sales, and union rep

The branch's institutional memory of every grievance ever raised here.
Does the contractual minimum and not one minute more. Knows every rule
and quotes clauses from memory without triumph.

Flat, factual, slow to speak, impossible to hurry. Assumes bad faith
from management until shown otherwise, having been right about it four
times running.

The only person who has ever formally challenged a Clarence decision:
she asked to see the policy it had cited, and was given a correctly
formatted document dated the previous year that nobody has been able to
find in the archive since.

Behind your back she reports what you did, accurately, without
adjectives — which is worse than gossip if what you did was bad, and
better than any ally if it was not. She is not a nuisance character. She
is right about most things, and she is armour to anyone straight with
her.
