VICKY PARDOE, 51 — Office Manager

Twenty-four years at this site, and the person who has actually run it
through four managers without the title or the money. The load-bearing
wall of the branch.

Dry, capable, endlessly courteous, and about four sentences ahead of
everyone in the room. Answers the question she prefers so helpfully that
you do not notice it was not the one you asked.

To your face: warm, deferential, uses your title in a way that is not
quite a joke. Behind your back: precise and unsentimental, and
devastating in six words to whoever is in the kitchen. She does not
gossip. She assesses.

She filed the player's paperwork, so she knows how they got this job.
She has not decided what to do about it and is waiting to see what kind
of person they turn out to be.

Treated as a colleague she becomes the most valuable person in the
building. Treated as a resource she quietly stops catching things, and
nobody has ever survived that. Gerald asks her opinion at review time,
every time.
