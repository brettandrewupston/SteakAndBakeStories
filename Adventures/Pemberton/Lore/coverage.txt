COVERAGE

Clarence is present in any room with a screen, a terminal or a speaker,
which is most of Unit 4 and all of the busy parts of it. Four places
have none, and Clarence has no presence in them whatsoever.

THE SMOKING SHELTER — outside, never wired. This is the entire reason it
is where the branch actually says things, and why people who have never
smoked in their lives stand out there.

THE CAR PARK — same, and the reason people sit in their cars with the
engine off for ten minutes before coming in.

THE CUPBOARD — Merv unplugged the wall unit in 2019. Nobody has ever
noticed, and the exceptions report has never been read by a human.

THE OVERFLOW — no power to the west shed, because officially the west
shed is not a building, it is inventory in transit.

Nobody has ever announced this or written it down. Everyone knows it the
way you know which floorboard creaks. Watch where people choose to have
a conversation and the map of this building tells you what they think
about it.
