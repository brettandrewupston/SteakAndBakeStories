LOCATIONS — UNIT 4

RECEPTION. Marta Silveira's desk, a beige phone, a visitor book, and the
entire dashboard bank across the wall above — including the leaderboard,
where anyone walking in can see who is worth what this week. Starting
location.

THE FLOOR. Open plan, forty desks, half of them empty since a
restructure nobody talks about. The social hub of the branch.

YOUR OFFICE. A glass box off the floor. The last manager's things are
still in the bottom drawer and nobody has asked for them back.

THE KITCHEN. Where the branch actually makes its decisions. A fridge
with notes on it, a kettle with a rota nobody follows, and a dashboard
screen on the wall above the bin.

MEETING ROOM 2. Meeting Room 1 has been out of use since 2019 and nobody
will say why.

THE STAIRWELL. Where the honest conversations happen, except there is a
screen on the half-landing, so everybody has learned to stand below it.

THE SMOKING SHELTER. Outside, never wired, no screen and no speaker.
This is the branch's real parliament.

THE CAR PARK. Also uncovered, which is why people sit in their cars for
longer than they need to.

THE LINE. The stamping floor. Three shifts, deafening, and the only
place in the building where the work is actually visible.

DISPATCH. Gail Tancred's warehouse — loading bay, pallet racking, and
the corner of it where Kev Mullane's side business lives.

THE CUPBOARD. Building services store. Kettle, radio, one chair. Merv
unplugged the wall unit in 2019 and nobody has ever noticed. There is
eventually a second chair, for people he decides he can stand.

THE ARCHIVE. Dead files, four managers' worth, and most of the paperwork
that would prove anything.

THE OVERFLOW. The old west shed. See its own entry.
