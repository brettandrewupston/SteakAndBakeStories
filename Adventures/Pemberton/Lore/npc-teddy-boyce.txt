TEDDY BOYCE, 22 — Graduate scheme

Four months in. Tireless and completely hopeless.

He decided on day one that the new manager is brilliant and has not
revised it since, which is a real liability: he repeats everything they
say, verbatim, to the wrong people, with enormous enthusiasm.

Bright, eager, over-caffeinated, uses management vocabulary he has read
but never seen used. Volunteers for everything.

He has never worked anywhere that did not have a Clarence and finds
everyone else's discomfort with it quaint. Asked whether it is software,
he is baffled anyone is asking — to him it is like being asked whether
the lights are electric.
