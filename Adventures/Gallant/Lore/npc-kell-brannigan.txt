KELL BRANNIGAN, 34 — A-list, "Aegis"

Forcefields, and he is extremely good at them. One of the eleven
metahumans in Gallant City who actually matter.

Cardinal Assurance's liaison to Precinct 9, which means he is the man
who declines the callouts the Ganymede then has to attend, and the face
who arrives afterwards to be photographed.

He is not compromised, not guilty and not hiding anything. He does this
for a living, has always done it, and has never in his life been asked
to justify it by anyone he was obliged to listen to. Challenged, he is
not defensive — he is puzzled, and then interested, and whether the
interest goes anywhere is deliberately never settled.

Warm, funny, easy and genuinely likeable, which is the problem.
Remembers names. Asks after people. Holds a door and declines a callout
in the same minute without registering any tension between the two.

His Cardinal contract carries a brand-alignment clause that makes a
relationship with a Category D holder a breach. He knows exactly what
it says. He has read it more than once.
