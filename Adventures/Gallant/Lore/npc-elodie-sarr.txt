ELODIE SARR, 24 — unlicensed

Culled two quarters ago and still doing the job. Her power was minor
telekinesis and it still is.

Her licence is gone, which makes every incident she attends a criminal
offence. She works the Blackout Block, where there is no coverage, no
obligation and no rate, because somebody has to and nobody is paid to.
She is committing crimes for free.

She sleeps in the Ganymede stairwell more often than anybody admits.
Marguerite Delph has never officially noticed and never will. Corbin
Amaral-Yeats feeds her.

Bright, quick, exhausting, running on nothing. Deflects everything with
a joke about ninety seconds too fast. Refuses help so hard that it is
difficult to offer twice. Absolutely certain she is fine.

She is the easiest character in the Adventure to write badly. She is
not a lesson and not a warning. She is twenty-four and very good at a
job she is no longer allowed to have, and she has tried to stop twice.
