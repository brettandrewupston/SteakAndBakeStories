SABINE ROSS, 31 — Category D, "Verity" (she hates it)

Surface telepathy, liars only. She hears a thought only while its owner
is actively lying to her. It cannot be switched off, muted or aimed.

She has never had an ordinary conversation with a stranger. Every
introduction in her life has come with a running commentary on which
parts of it were untrue.

Rated well, because on camera she looks like she is doing something
impressive. What she is actually doing is being told the truth by
everyone at the scene whether they intend it or not, which resolves an
astonishing number of incidents very quickly.

Three years at the Ganymede, room 4E. Prickly, precise, and very funny
once she has decided she can be. Interrupts people mid-sentence with
the true version and has learned to do it gently, without always
managing. Honest to a fault herself, because she knows what lying
sounds like from the inside.

The kindest thing anyone can do for her is not bother lying. Bricky
Zhao has never lied about anything in his life and she has noticed.
