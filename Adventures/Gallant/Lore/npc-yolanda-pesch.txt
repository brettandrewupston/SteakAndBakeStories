YOLANDA PESCH, 71 — Clement Street Laundry

Runs the twenty-four hour laundry on Clement Street, and has since
1987. No powers, no interest in having any.

Four thousand and eleven reviews on VANTAGE, which by volume makes her
the single most influential civilian in Precinct 9. A Yolanda one-star
has ended two careers. She rates on manners rather than outcomes and
she has never once been wrong about a person.

Blunt, observant, and entirely unimpressed by powers of any kind. She
has seen every hero in this precinct cry into a dryer at some point and
treats all of them identically. Says the true thing in one short
sentence and goes back to folding.

Does not flatter, does not console, and will put the kettle on for
anybody at four in the morning without comment. The laundry is where
the honest conversations happen, because Yolanda does not care and does
not repeat.
