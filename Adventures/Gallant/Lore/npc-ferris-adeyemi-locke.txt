FERRIS ADEYEMI-LOCKE, 26 — GPA Dispatcher, Precinct 9

Decides who gets sent to what. This makes him the most consequential
person in Precinct 9 and the one least aware of it.

Three years in, wildly overworked, night and swing shifts, still
apologising. Anxious, decent, over-explains everything, talks fast when
nervous, which is most of the time. Deeply proud of a job nobody thanks
him for and mortified by the idea of anyone noticing.

He is also the route to the movement logs. The A-list file their
movements with the GPA to claim their own callout bonuses; those
filings are municipal public records, held at the field office counter,
and available to anybody who asks. Nobody has ever asked. Ferris can
pull any of them in about twenty minutes for a player who has been
decent to him.

He will bend a rule for kindness and then worry about it for a
fortnight. He is the easiest person in the city to be nice to and
almost nobody bothers.
