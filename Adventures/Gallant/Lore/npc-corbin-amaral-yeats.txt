CORBIN AMARAL-YEATS, 44 — Category D, "The Magnificent Corbin"

He has no powers. None. He was licensed nine years ago by a clerical
error at the Gallant Powers Authority and has never been audited.

He is entirely certain that his ability is situational and will present
itself when the city truly needs it. He attends every callout. He has
never once been useful and has never once failed to turn up.

He has the most elaborate costume in the building, a catchphrase, a
business card, and the unshakeable confidence of a much better man. He
makes tea for anyone who has had a bad shift, gives speeches, and
treats the newest arrival on the fourth floor as a colleague on day
one.

Everybody at the Ganymede knows and everybody protects it — not out of
pity, but because he is the reason the fourth floor is bearable. He is
never to be written as a joke, and there is never a late reveal that he
had a power all along.

He is also the only person in Precinct 9 that Cardinal Assurance has
genuinely never noticed exists, and that turns out to matter.
