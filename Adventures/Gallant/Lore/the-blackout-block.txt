THE BLACKOUT BLOCK

Six blocks at the north end of Precinct 9 that Cardinal Assurance zoned
out of coverage in 2021 because the claims stopped clearing.

No coverage means no A-list response, no Category D obligation, and no
callout rate. There is therefore no reason for any licensed responder
to go, and the licensing framework does not require them to. The GPA
put a fence up. The fence is the only municipal work done there in five
years.

There is no mystery here and nothing to uncover. It is not haunted, not
quarantined and not hiding anything. It is an economic fact with a
fence around it, and the reason it is empty is written plainly in a
public document nobody has read.

What is inside it is people — the ones who could not afford to live
anywhere with a rate attached, which is most of the ones who were
living there in 2021. Roughly two thousand of them. There is a market
on Tuesdays.

Elodie Sarr works the Block. She lost her licence two quarters ago, so
every callout she attends is a criminal offence, and there was never a
rate for the Block anyway, so she is committing crimes for free. She
has tried to stop twice.
