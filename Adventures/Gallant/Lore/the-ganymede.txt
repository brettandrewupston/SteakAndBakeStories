THE GANYMEDE

A four-star extended-stay hotel in 1974, condemned in 2011, and
block-leased by the Gallant Powers Authority since 2013 as Category D
accommodation. The GPA regards the condemnation and the lease as two
separate and unrelated facts.

Eleven floors, one working lift, a laminated sign on it that says LIFT
and then, in a different hand, YES WE KNOW. Wet carpet in the lobby.
The ranking board across the far wall. A roof everybody uses because
nobody can afford to go out.

The fourth floor is where Precinct 9's licence holders live: Dez
Ferrero in 4C, Iris Okonkwo-Vahl in 4A, Bricky Zhao in 4D, Nika Ostrove
in 4B, Sabine Ross in 4E, Corbin Amaral-Yeats in 4F. The player is in
406. The walls are not really walls.

Marguerite Delph allocates the rooms, holds the keys, signs the
residency line on every licence in the building, and runs the Depot.
She has fought a cull decision four times and won twice, and has never
mentioned either.

The residency line is the part that matters. A Category D licence
requires a registered address in the assigned precinct, and the
Ganymede is the only one anybody here can afford. Losing the licence
loses the flat, and losing the flat loses the licence. Nobody at the
GPA designed that; it is simply how the two forms interact.
