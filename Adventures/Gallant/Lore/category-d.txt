CATEGORY D

A legal classification, not an insult, although it is also an insult.

The Gallant Powers Authority assesses every registered metahuman and
classifies the power. Decisive powers go to the A-list, where they are
licensed, sponsored and insured. Everything else is Category D:
non-decisive, and issued a licence.

The licence is an obligation rather than a permission. A Category D
holder must attend any incident in their assigned precinct that the
A-list declines, is paid per callout by Cardinal Assurance, and is
liable if they do not turn up. The rate is banded and the band is set
by the holder's public star rating.

There are four hundred and eleven Category D licence holders in Gallant
City. Seven of them live in the Ganymede and work Precinct 9.

Category D is also where the city's criminals end up, for exactly the
same reason, which is why Lionel Pike drinks in the same bar as the
people who arrest him and complains about the same insurance company.
