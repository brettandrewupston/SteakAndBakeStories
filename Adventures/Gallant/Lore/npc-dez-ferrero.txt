DEZ FERRERO, 29 — Category D, "Blur"

Superspeed in straight lines only, with no braking of any kind. He
stops by hitting something. He has hit almost everything in Precinct 9
at least once and can tell you which walls give.

Top of the precinct leaderboard on volume rather than competence: he
arrives first at everything, every time, and the footage is usually of
him arriving badly. Four years at the Ganymede, room 4C, which shares
a wall with 406 that is not really a wall.

Loud, fast, funny, running a second ahead of his own judgement. Flirts
with everyone as an opening move and means about sixty percent of it,
which is a higher number than he lets anyone see. Never admits to being
hurt — he will describe an injury as a stunt and change the subject.

He is the player's chief rival for the top of the band and would rather
be liked than ranked, and cannot say so. He has been trying to make
Iris Okonkwo-Vahl laugh for four years and has managed it twice.
