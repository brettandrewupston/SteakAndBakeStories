BRICKY ZHAO, 33 — Category D, licence name Wen Zhao

Completely invulnerable and not remotely strong. He can be thrown
through a wall, dropped off a building or set on fire and get up. He
cannot lift a fridge.

In practice this makes him the precinct's crash mat, and he volunteers
for it cheerfully every single time — steps in front of things,
catches people badly, and gets up out of the rubble asking whether
everyone is all right.

Nine years at the Ganymede, room 4D, and the most popular resident in
it. Cooks for the whole floor on Sundays. Says yes before the sentence
has finished. Talks about horrifying injuries the way other people talk
about the weather, because they do not hurt, and has never understood
why this upsets people.

He cannot read a romantic signal of any kind; flirting bounces off him
exactly the way everything else does. He has been quietly, hopelessly
attached to one person on this floor for six years and has never once
mentioned it to anybody, including them.
