LOCATIONS — PRECINCT 9

GANYMEDE LOBBY. Wet carpet, one lift, and the ranking board across the
far wall — seven names, live stars, updated every ninety seconds where
anybody walking in can see them. Starting location.

THE ROOF. Where the fourth floor actually lives. Deck chairs, a dead
satellite dish, a view of Altitude lit up eleven blocks away, and the
only place in the building where nobody is filming.

YOUR ROOM. Four-oh-six. The window does not open. The wall shared with
4C is not really a wall.

FOURTH FLOOR CORRIDOR. Six doors and a lift that works sometimes.
Everything anybody says on this floor is heard by at least one person
it was not meant for.

THE LAUNDRY. Yolanda Pesch's, on Clement Street, open all night. The
second hub of the Adventure and the place where the honest
conversations happen, because Yolanda does not care and does not
repeat.

GPA FIELD OFFICE. Ferris Adeyemi-Locke's dispatch desk, the rota board,
and a public records counter that has been open to the public since
1998 and has been visited eleven times.

CLEMENT STREET. The strip. Where the incidents are, where the cameras
are, and where a Category D holder's entire income is decided.

THE ROTUNDA. Transit hub, twelve thousand people an hour, and the worst
possible place in the precinct for anything to go wrong.

CARDINAL PLAZA. The tower's public forecourt: a fountain, a
twelve-metre statue of Halo, and security who know every face on the
fourth floor by sight.

ALTITUDE. The rooftop bar at the top of the Cardinal tower. A-list and
press. Everyone at the Ganymede can see it lit up from the roof and
none of them has ever been inside.

THE DEPOT. GPA impound. Confiscated equipment, and Marguerite Delph
selling it back to the people it was taken from.

THE WALK-IN. Twenty-four hour clinic on Clement. Stopped asking
Category D holders how it happened some years ago.

THE BLACKOUT BLOCK. Six blocks with no coverage. See its own entry.
