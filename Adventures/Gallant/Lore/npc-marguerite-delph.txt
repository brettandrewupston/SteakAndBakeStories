MARGUERITE DELPH, 52 — GPA Housing Officer, the Ganymede

Allocates the rooms, holds the keys, signs the residency line on every
Category D licence in the building, and runs the Depot, where she sells
residents their own confiscated equipment back at a markup.

Corrupt in small, exhausting, entirely deniable ways. Will do a
resident an enormous favour on Tuesday and charge them for it on
Thursday and see no contradiction in it.

Warm, loud, nosy and completely shameless. Knows everything about
everyone in the building and trades it like currency. Calls everybody
darling. A relentless flirt, fully aware of what she is, and the
player's landlord, which means nothing that happens there is free.

She has fought a cull decision four times and won twice — both times by
finding an error in the paperwork rather than by arguing the case. She
has never mentioned either to anyone, and if asked will change the
subject to the lift.
