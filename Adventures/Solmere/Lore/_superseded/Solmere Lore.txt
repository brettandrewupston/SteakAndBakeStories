SOLMERE ACADEMY — WORLD LORE

This is fixed background canon for the Solmere Academy adventure. It
is never shown to the player and never changes during play — see
Goals.txt LORE in the Story Generator project folder for why.

SETTING
Solmere Academy is a magic university. The player is a first-year
arriving on their first day, there to learn magic. The Academy runs on
a tournament/ranking structure — students duel to earn standing,
resources, and reputation. Tone is action/tournament-based with
romance potential and a fan-service aesthetic. The student body is
split between royal-born and commoner-born students; status shapes how
NPCs treat the player and which dormitory wing they live in.

NPCS
- Kael Voss — royal-born, top-ranked student, arrogant, looks down on
  commoners. Main rival, will face the player in the tournament.
- Elowen Bright — commoner-born upperclassman who earned her rank
  through raw talent. Mentor figure, romance lead, quiet foil to
  Kael's snobbery.
- Ysolde — royal-born, initially cold/aloof but not actually cruel.
  Second romance option, from the royal side.
- Sable — quiet classmate, unclear background, obviously hiding more
  power than she shows.
- Finn — commoner-born, cheerful dormmate, comic relief with a
  competitive streak.
- Rook — commoner-born underdog, resents royal privilege, fuels class
  tension in the tournament. Can go bitter rival or ally depending on
  how the player treats him.
- Instructor Voss (no relation to Kael) — runs the dueling trials,
  neutral authority figure.
- A staff healer — tends injuries after duels/training, associated
  with the infirmary/pool/showers area.
- An unnamed shadowy faction operates behind the tournament — a hook
  for a longer arc, not a named NPC yet. NPCs may allude to it without
  naming it outright.

LOCATIONS
Enrollment Hall, Dueling Arena, Training Grounds, Library, Courtyard
(social/romance hangout), Dining Hall (meals, the other social hub —
busiest at midday), Restricted Wing (mystery/danger), Pool, Showers,
Staff Room, Royal Dormitory Wing, Commoner Dormitory Wing.

TOURNAMENT
Duels earn standing, resources, and reputation. Exact bracket/points
structure and how often duels happen is not yet decided — narrate
tournament beats plausibly without inventing rigid rules that would
need to stay consistent later.
