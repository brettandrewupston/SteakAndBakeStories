SOLMERE ACADEMY — ADVENTURE CONTENT
===========================================================
Last updated: 24/08/2026
First Adventure built for the Story Generator. Architecture/decisions
live in the parent folder's Goals.txt and Features.txt — this file is
just this Adventure's canon (premise, NPCs, map, setup fields).

PREMISE
----------
Player is a first-year arriving at Solmere Academy on their first day,
there to learn magic. The Academy runs on a tournament/ranking
structure — students duel to earn standing, resources, and
reputation, giving a built-in reason for escalating action through the
story rather than it feeling bolted on. Action/tournament-based, with
romance potential and fan-service tone.

Social structure: the student body is split between royal-born and
commoner-born students — status shapes how NPCs initially treat the
player and which dormitory wing they start in.

CONTENT APPROACH
--------------------
Default narration doesn't proactively write explicit content (fade-
to-black is the default instinct), but won't refuse or block if the
user's own input pushes it there. Same non-negotiable minor-safety
layer underneath regardless — see Safety Layer.txt (Image Generator)
and Goals.txt SAFETY, unaffected by this.

NPCs (12 — every category below has at least one named NPC now)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Appearance lines are for the Images system (Stage A baseline portraits
— see Goals.txt IMAGES) — free-text in the same style as the Image
Generator's character description field, but deliberately clothing-
free: clothing comes from the Category (below) via the standardized
STANDARD UNIFORMS block instead of being hand-written per NPC, so
every character sharing a Category reads with consistent uniform
language. The authoritative wording for both the per-NPC Appearance
lines and the uniform text lives in Story Definitions/Solmere.json; this
file is the readable reference to the same content. Every character is a
clearly adult person; the two-layer image safety system enforces this in
code
regardless, but these are written unambiguously adult on purpose too.

Personality lines are separate from the bio/role summary below — bio is
who a character is (role, background, relationships), Personality is
how they talk (word choice, tone, speech habits), fed to the narration
AI specifically to keep NPCs from reading interchangeably in dialogue
(see Goals.txt "Narration/dialogue voice differentiation").

STANDARD UNIFORMS (Stage A baseline portraits)
---------------------------------------------------
Six categories, each combined with an NPC's Appearance line and a
generic neutral background at generation time — not written per-NPC,
so changing one line here updates every NPC sharing that Category.
Working defaults; the story definition holds the authoritative wording.
- royal-student-male / royal-student-female: tailored deep navy coat
  (fitted coat-dress for female) with gold trim and an embroidered
  royal crest on the chest, white high-collared shirt/blouse
  underneath, polished boots, formal and pristine.
- commoner-student-male / commoner-student-female: plain grey wool
  jacket over a simple shirt/blouse, practical trousers or skirt,
  sturdy worn boots, no crest, practical and unadorned.
- staff-male / staff-female: dark charcoal formal robes (or fitted
  coat for female) with the Academy crest, structured high collar,
  authoritative and pristine.

LOCATIONS
-----------------

- Library
- Courtyard (social/romance hangout)
- Dining Hall (meals — the Academy's other social hub, busiest at Midday)
- Restricted Wing (mystery/danger) — not drawn on the map
- Pool
- Showers
- Staff Room
- Royal Dormitory Wing
- Commoner Dormitory Wing

Hidden locations are left off the map. The Restricted Wing's existence is
a discovery the story gates (see THE LADDER, wing_found), and the journal
already conceals that milestone — drawing the room on a map the player can
open from turn one would hand it straight over. The engine tolerates a
location with no element on the map: the room simply gets no "you are
here" marker while the player is in it.

The map is illustrated art — `Maps/Solmere Map.webp`, hosted, with the PNG
master in `AssetsNotHosted/Masters/`. `Maps/Solmere Map.svg` is the wrapper
around it: the image plus one invisible circular anchor per room, each
carrying the `loc-<slug>` id the engine looks up to place the marker. The
room names are drawn into the art, so moving a building means moving its
anchor to match.

Conventions this table follows:
- No more than two NPCs share a location in a phase, the Dining Hall
  excepted. An authoring guide, not an engine rule — roaming NPCs can
  still push a beat past it.
- The Dining Hall is the exception because a crowded refectory is the
  point of it: three at Midday, two at Evening, one at Morning. It is
  empty in the Afternoon and at Night — an empty hall after hours is a
  place to meet someone unobserved.
- The Enrollment Hall is empty in every phase; it's an arrivals hall.
- The Restricted Wing is Ysolde, Night, and nothing else.
- The Showers are scheduled once (Seraphine, Midday) and otherwise reached
  through roamLocations, which eleven of the twelve NPCs list — so anyone
  might be there and nobody reliably is.

THE LADDER (tournament structure)
--------------------------------------
Designed 23/08/2026. A standing ranking ladder, not a knockout bracket:
every student holds a rank, duels are frequent and can be sought out or
forced on the player, and losing costs rank rather than ending anyone's
season. Chosen over a bracket because the premise already describes duels
earning "standing, resources and reputation," which is a ladder — and
because a bracket yields only about four duels across a whole playthrough
and strands an early loser with most of the story left and nothing to
pursue. Duels between the bands below are unlimited and emergent; the
engine only tracks the band crossings.

The arc data itself lives in Story Definitions/Solmere.json (see Goals.txt
STORY ARC & ENDINGS for the engine mechanic); this is the readable
reference to the same content.

Bands, in order — each crossed once:
- ranked — first sanctioned duel fought in front of an instructor, the
  player is entered on the ladder near the bottom. Ends Act I.
- contender — wins up out of the lower ranks into the upper tier, the
  handful actually in contention. Changes who trains with them, who
  learns their name, and who now wants them beaten.
- challenge (earned / denied) — petitions to challenge the top-ranked
  student. Denied is final for the season and puts the title out of
  reach. Either outcome ends Act II.
- title (won / lost) — the title duel in the Dueling Arena in front of
  the whole Academy. The last duel of the season either way.
- washed_out — finished as a competitor: a run of losses, barred from
  the ladder, or walking away from it. The other route into Act III.

Side thread, off the ladder:
- wing_found — gets past the Restricted Wing's wards and comes out
  holding hard evidence that the rankings are being arranged from
  outside the Academy. Needs `ranked` first.
- faction_faced (exposed / buried) — forces a confrontation with
  whoever is arranging them. Exposed ends the story; buried does not —
  it means living inside a system the player now knows is rigged.
  This is what the previously unnamed shadowy faction is for.

Acts: I until ranked (an unranked first-year being sized up), II until
challenge or washed_out (the climb), III after (where the season ends).

Journal visibility: the four ladder bands are listed from turn one — the
ladder is public knowledge in-world and a player should know a title
exists. wing_found and washed_out are flagged hidden, so neither is
listed until it actually happens: the Restricted Wing's existence is the
discovery, and listing "out of contention" as something ahead of the
player would read as an instruction rather than a risk. faction_faced
needs no flag of its own — concealment is inherited from wing_found.
Each milestone carries a player-facing `journal` line separate from the
AI-facing `description`.

ENDINGS
-----------
Six, one primary per playthrough. Death and expulsion are reachable from
the first turn; the rest resolve the season.
- Killed at Solmere — no continue, New Story only.
- Expelled from Solmere — no continue.
- What the Ladder Was For — faction_faced:exposed. Continuable.
- Champion of Solmere — title:won. Continuable.
- Broken on the Arena Floor — title:lost. Continuable.
- One of the Many — the season closes without the title, after a denied
  challenge or washing out. Continuable.
Romance has no ending of its own by design — the ending beat closes out
whoever the player ended on strong or bad terms with, whatever the
ending was.
