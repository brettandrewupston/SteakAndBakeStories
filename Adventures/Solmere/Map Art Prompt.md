# Solmere Map — image prompt

Paste the block below into ChatGPT (image generation). Ask for **landscape,
1536 × 1024**. Save the result as `Solmere Map.png` in this folder.

The layout below matches `Solmere Map.svg` room for room, so the invisible
anchors that drive the "you are here" marker can be placed straight onto the
art. The Restricted Wing is absent on purpose — hidden locations stay off the
map.

---

## The prompt

> A hand-drawn bird's-eye estate map of a magic university campus, in the style
> of an antique ink-and-wash architectural plan on aged parchment. Muted sepia,
> warm cream and faded gold, with fine brown ink linework and soft watercolour
> washes. Slightly tilted three-quarter perspective on the buildings, flat
> ground plan underneath. No people, no vehicles, no modern objects. Landscape
> orientation.
>
> The campus is arranged around one large open courtyard at the dead centre,
> with pale stone paths radiating out from it to every other building. Place
> the buildings exactly as follows:
>
> - **Top left:** a long stone dormitory block, plain and unadorned. Label:
>   *Commoner Dormitory Wing*
> - **Top centre:** a grand arched gatehouse and entrance hall, the way in from
>   outside the campus. Label: *Enrollment Hall*
> - **Top right:** an ornate dormitory block with gold-trimmed roofs and
>   banners, clearly the grander of the two. Label: *Royal Dormitory Wing*
> - **Middle left:** a tall library with high windows and a domed roof. Label:
>   *Library*
> - **Dead centre:** a wide open courtyard with paving, a fountain and a few
>   trees. Label: *Courtyard*
> - **Middle right:** a circular tiered dueling amphitheatre, open to the sky.
>   Label: *Dueling Arena*
> - **Lower left:** a long hall with a steep pitched roof and chimneys, clearly
>   a refectory. Label: *Dining Hall*
> - **Lower centre:** an open field of practice yards, targets and dummies.
>   Label: *Training Grounds*
> - **Lower right, three smaller buildings grouped close together:** a
>   rectangular swimming pool building labelled *Pool*, a small bathhouse
>   labelled *Showers*, and a small staff lodge labelled *Staff Room*
>
> Every label is hand-lettered in a clear serif script in dark brown ink, on a
> small parchment ribbon or plaque beside its building, and must be spelled
> exactly as written above. A small compass rose in one corner. A thin plain
> border. No other text anywhere on the image — no title, no legend, no key.

---

## Checks before it's usable

- Every one of the eleven labels present and spelled correctly.
- Nothing that reads as a restricted, forbidden or ruined wing.
- No invented extra labelled buildings — unlabelled scenery is fine.
- Rooms roughly where the list puts them, so the anchors line up.

If the lettering keeps coming out garbled, re-run the prompt with the final
paragraph replaced by *"No text or lettering anywhere on the image"* — the room
names can then be drawn on as SVG text instead, which also lets them recolour
with the parchment skin.
