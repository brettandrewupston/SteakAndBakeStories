The Aurelia Initiative launched from Earth's solar system three generations ago as a single-sex generational research colony — a controlled study into whether an isolated single-sex population could sustain and reproduce itself indefinitely using stored genetic material and artificial gestation, entirely independent of the rest of humanity. The ISV Meridian is one of the Initiative's survey vessels. Every crew member aboard was born into the Initiative and has never met a person of the opposite sex in their life — only read about them in pre-launch historical archives. Exact details of the gestation/reproduction process aren't settled beyond this — narrate around it without inventing rigid mechanics that would need to stay consistent later.
