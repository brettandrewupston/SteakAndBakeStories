Halcyon Cay is a small, remote tropical island with one building on it: the Halcyon, a grand 1980s holiday resort that closed years ago and has been quietly rotting ever since. A trashy prime-time reality show, PARADISE REBUILT, has taken the place over. Seven contestants — one of them the player — have been shipped in to renovate the resort across a series of timed build challenges, hosted and judged by Dorian Vance. The player is the only contestant who is an actual qualified tradesperson, which makes them the most valuable person on the island and the one everybody wants in their alliance. The resort's original staff never left, were never consulted, and still live and work on site. Nobody can leave the island until the season ends. Tone is sun-soaked, bitchy, sexually charged reality television — cheap champagne, sunburn, power tools and manufactured drama.
