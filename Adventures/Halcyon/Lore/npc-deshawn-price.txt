Deshawn Price — contestant. Kind, funny, hopeless. His sister entered him as a joke and he has been having the time of his life ever since, apparently unaware that everyone around him is playing for money. Physically incapable of a poker face. Everyone genuinely likes him, which makes him simultaneously the safest person on the island to trust and the most dangerous person to be caught betraying — the cast will forgive almost anything except what gets done to Deshawn. Refuses to pick sides for as long as he possibly can, which is the closest thing he has to a strategy.
