RESORT LOBBY — grand, faded, water-stained ceiling and a dead chandelier. Where contestants arrive, where Dorian makes announcements, and where challenge results are read out.
POOL DECK — the social hub. Sun beds, a green-tinged pool that has only just been re-filled, and cameras on every side. Almost every alliance on the island is formed or broken here.
POOL BAR — reopened by production on day one because drunk contestants make better television. Bastien Roche pours heavily and remembers everything.
CONTESTANT VILLAS — the strip of guest bungalows the contestants sleep in. Thin walls, doors that don't all lock, and no cameras inside the rooms — officially.
STAFF QUARTERS — the original staff block behind the kitchens. Off-limits to the crew by contract. Where Renata, Josefa, Camille, Teo and Bastien actually live, and the one place on the island the show cannot reach.
THE SITE — the half-gutted wing under renovation. Scaffolding, dust, exposed joists and whatever this phase's challenge is. Dangerous when people are careless, and people are careless constantly.
TOOL SHED — Teo's domain, and the only building on the island with a decent lock. Also, inevitably, where people go when they want ten minutes without a camera.
CONFESSIONAL BOOTH — a converted linen store with a chair, a lamp and one locked-off camera. Contestants speak to it alone. Nobody lies in here, which is the problem.
BEACH — the far side of the cay, a fifteen-minute walk through the palms. The only place far enough out that a conversation might genuinely be private.
OUTDOOR SHOWERS — open-air rinse-off between the pool and the villas, screened by slatted timber that has warped badly over the years.
MANAGER'S OFFICE — Renata's office. Ledgers, keys, thirty years of the resort's paperwork, and one drawer she keeps locked.
THE SUNKEN VILLA — the resort's west end. Fenced, flooded, excluded from the renovation, and not up for discussion.
