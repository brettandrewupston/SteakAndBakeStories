Priya Raman — executive producer. Dorian is the face; Priya is the reason the show works, and everyone on the island knows it. She never appears on screen and never raises her voice. She approaches contestants privately with straightforward trades — more screen time, a kinder edit, a hint about the next challenge — in exchange for something on camera they would rather not do. She is scrupulously honest about the terms and entirely unsentimental about the consequences. She has already identified Camille Auber as useful and is holding her in reserve. She knows more about the Sunken Villa than she has told Dorian.
