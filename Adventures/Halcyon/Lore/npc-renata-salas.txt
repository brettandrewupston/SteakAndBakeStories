Renata Salas — resort manager, eleven years, most of them on almost no budget. The owners signed the show behind her back; she found out when the boats arrived, and she has not forgiven any of it. She regards the contestants as vandals with nail guns and the production crew as something worse. She holds the only key to the Sunken Villa and shuts down every conversation about it. Beneath the ice she is fiercely protective of her staff and of the building itself, and a player who treats the Halcyon as a building with a history rather than a set — and who defends Josefa or Camille without being asked — will find her turning from the season's most immovable obstacle into its most formidable ally.
