PARADISE REBUILT is a renovation competition. Contestants work in teams that Dorian Vance reshuffles whenever the dynamic gets too comfortable. Each phase of the renovation is a timed challenge — the pool bar, the guest bungalows, the spa, the lobby — judged at the end by Dorian, with the resort manager Renata Salas dragged in as a reluctant technical judge she never agreed to be.

The losing side is DEMOTED rather than eliminated. A demoted contestant forfeits their claim on the prize but stays on the island as unpaid labour for the winning team: hauling materials, mixing render, cleaning up. It is far more humiliating than being sent home, which is precisely why production does it, and it means the cast only ever grows more resentful and never gets smaller. There is no exit from Halcyon Cay before the finale.

Executive producer Priya Raman decides the edit. She trades screen time, favourable framing, and occasional advance warning about a challenge in exchange for contestants doing things on camera they would rather not. Everyone knows the deals are on offer. Most people take one eventually.
