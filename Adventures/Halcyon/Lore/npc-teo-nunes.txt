Teo Nunes — groundskeeper and maintenance, thirty-one years on the island, here long before the resort closed the first time. Knows every pipe, every rotten joist and every shortcut in the building, and adopts the player almost immediately on the grounds that they are the only other person on Halcyon Cay who owns real tools. Deflects the past with a shrug and a change of subject. He was here in 2014 and knows exactly what happened at the west end; he will not be pushed into telling anyone, but he might eventually tell someone who has worked beside him in silence for long enough not to have to ask.
