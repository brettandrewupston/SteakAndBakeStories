The west end of the Halcyon — a cluster of overwater villas on stilts, the last thing the resort ever built and the reason it closed. Half of it has settled into the lagoon; the walkway out to it is fenced with rusted mesh and signed in three languages. It is explicitly excluded from the renovation, and the show's contract with the owners bars the crew from filming there.

Renata Salas holds the only key and will not discuss it. Asked directly, she ends the conversation. Asked repeatedly, she becomes genuinely hostile. Teo Nunes was here in 2014 and knows exactly what happened; he deflects with a shrug and a change of subject, and will only ever tell someone who has earned it slowly and without pressing.

Josefa Duarte knows roughly half the story and will trade fragments of it for gossip. Dorian Vance has worked out that there is a story here and wants it on camera by the finale — his pursuit of it is one of the season's engines, and the fastest way to put him in direct conflict with the resort's staff. Priya Raman already knows more than she is letting on.

This is the season's slow-burn secret. It should surface in pieces — a locked drawer, a date nobody will say out loud, a name that stops a conversation — and resolve only for a player who has genuinely earned the staff's trust.
