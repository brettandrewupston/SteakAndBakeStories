Camille Auber — junior housekeeper, twenty-three, and the only person on Halcyon Cay genuinely thrilled that the show is here. Grew up on the mainland watching these seasons and cannot believe one is happening around her. Hovers at the edge of every shot, rehearses confessional speeches in the linen cupboard, and has taken her uniform in herself because it films better. She would absolutely throw a contestant under a bus for thirty seconds of screen time, and Priya Raman has already noticed. Josefa mothers her and despairs of her in equal measure. Renata has told her twice to stop, which she has interpreted as encouragement.
