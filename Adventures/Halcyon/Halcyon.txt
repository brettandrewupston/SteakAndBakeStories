PARADISE REBUILT (HALCYON CAY) — ADVENTURE CONTENT
===========================================================
Last updated: 22/08/2026
Third Adventure built for the Story Generator. Architecture/decisions
live in the parent folder's Goals.txt and Features.txt — this file is
just this Adventure's canon (premise, format, NPCs, map, setup fields).

Adventure id: halcyon. Display title: "Paradise Rebuilt". Files are
named Halcyon.* to match the id, consistent with Solmere/Meridian.

PREMISE
----------
Halcyon Cay is a small, remote tropical island with one building on it:
the Halcyon, a grand 1980s holiday resort closed and rotting for years.
A trashy prime-time reality show, PARADISE REBUILT, has taken it over.
Eight contestants — one of them the player — renovate the resort across
a series of timed build challenges, hosted and judged by Dorian Vance.

The player is the only contestant who is a qualified tradesperson. Every
alliance on the island needs their hands and everyone knows it, which is
the player's leverage and also what makes them a target.

The resort's original staff never left, were never consulted about the
show, and still live and work on site. The staff / contestant /
production divide is the island's main social fault line.

SHOW FORMAT
--------------
Contestants work in teams that Dorian reshuffles whenever the dynamic
gets comfortable. Each renovation phase is a timed, judged challenge
(pool bar, guest bungalows, spa, lobby). Renata Salas is dragged in as a
reluctant technical judge she never agreed to be.

The losing side is DEMOTED, not eliminated — a demoted contestant
forfeits their claim on the prize but stays on the island as unpaid
labour for the winners. Nobody ever leaves before the finale.

This is a deliberate design choice, not a softened elimination: it keeps
the cast at a constant fifteen NPCs, so the engine's static
currentStory.npcs roster needs no per-Adventure changes (no "eliminated"
flag, no shrinking roster, no resume-time reconciliation), while still
carrying real stakes and escalating resentment.

Executive producer Priya Raman controls the edit and trades screen time,
framing and challenge hints for on-camera behaviour contestants would
rather avoid.

THE CAMERAS (signature mechanic)
-----------------------------------
Cameras run almost everywhere, almost always, and every character knows
it. On camera they play their cast character; off camera they are
someone else. Whether a camera is currently rolling is live information
in every scene and characters react to it.

Implemented with no engine changes: each NPC's "personality" field —
which the prompt builder already injects verbatim — is written as an
explicit "On camera: X. Off camera: Y." pair, so every NPC carries a
two-layer persona for free. The rule itself is stated in
promptRules.setting under CAMERAS.

The Confessional Booth is a location: a converted linen store with one
locked-off camera where contestants speak alone and tell the truth about
each other.

CONTENT APPROACH
--------------------
Deliberately more permissive than Solmere/Meridian. Flirtation, hookups,
jealousy, betrayal and poolside sleaze are the expected texture of the
story rather than exceptions, and are written freely and explicitly when
a scene goes there; escalation within a scene still follows the player's
lead. All characters are written unambiguously adult. Same
non-negotiable minor-safety layer underneath regardless — see Safety
Layer.txt (Image Generator) and Goals.txt SAFETY, unaffected by this.

NPCs (15)
------------
Same authoring conventions as Solmere/Meridian: "identity" is the Stage
A baseline appearance line (clothing-free — clothing comes from the
Category via STANDARD UNIFORMS below), "bio" is who a character is,
"personality" is how they talk. Every character is a clearly adult
person.

Flat "npcs" array — this Adventure does NOT use npcSets/openingScenes.
The cast is mixed-gender by design and the player's gender does not
change the roster, so the gender-mirrored mechanism Meridian needs is
not applicable here, and the npcSets key-convention trap does not apply.

TOKENS: the engine matches NPCs by a plain case-sensitive substring scan
(text.includes(npc.token) / lastIndexOf, no word boundaries). Three
first names were changed at authoring time because their tokens would
have false-positived on ordinary capitalised words: Beau -> Cody
("Beautiful"), Wes -> Trev ("West"), Jo -> Josefa (any capitalised Jo*
word). All fifteen tokens were checked against each other, against the
twelve location names, and against a list of common sentence-initial
words before shipping.

PRODUCTION (3)
- Dorian Vance — host. Brilliant on camera, hollow off it; deliberately
  engineers arguments, leaks secrets, reshuffles teams. The story's
  drama engine: when a scene goes flat, Dorian is the character who
  walks in and breaks it. Has decided Cody is the season's star, that
  breaking the Quinn twins is his personal project, and that the Sunken
  Villa is his finale. Category: production-male.
- Priya Raman — executive producer. Controls the edit, never appears on
  screen, trades screen time and challenge hints for on-camera
  behaviour. Knows more about the Sunken Villa than Dorian does.
  Category: production-female.
- Milo Kessler — lead camera operator. Silent witness with a hard
  drive; answers a direct question honestly when the camera is down.
  Background-weight NPC. Category: production-male.

RESORT STAFF (5)
- Renata Salas — resort manager, eleven years. The owners signed the
  show behind her back. Holds the only key to the Sunken Villa and
  refuses to discuss it. Turns from the season's hardest obstacle into
  its most formidable ally if genuinely respected. Category:
  staff-female.
- Josefa Duarte — head of housekeeping, twenty-two years. Knows which
  doors don't lock and roughly half of what Renata is protecting.
  Trades information for gossip and basic courtesy. Best NPC to
  befriend early. Category: staff-female.
- Camille Auber — junior housekeeper, 23, desperate to be discovered by
  the cameras. Would throw a contestant under a bus for screen time.
  Priya has already spotted this. Category: staff-female.
- Teo Nunes — groundskeeper, thirty-one years on the island. Adopts the
  player as the only other person there with real tools. Was present in
  2014 and knows what happened at the west end. Category: staff-male.
- Bastien Roche — Pool Bar. Pours heavily, hears every 2am confession,
  repeats about half of them. Flirts with everyone. Category:
  staff-male.

CONTESTANTS (7 NPCs + the player)
- Vela Quinn — twin. The one keeping score; has privately decided only
  one Quinn wins and has not told her sister. Category:
  contestant-female.
- Nova Quinn — twin. Warmer, more genuine, starting to notice her
  sister's plans leave her holding the risk. Cracking her open breaks
  the twin bloc. Category: contestant-female.
- Cody Callahan — declared frontrunner by Dorian's edit. Enormously
  charming, cannot build anything; his work is quietly fixed overnight
  by other people. Terrified the player can tell. Category:
  contestant-male.
- Ingrid Holm — the only other real strategist. Offers the player a
  clean, honest hands-for-head alliance early and will honour it exactly
  as long as she needs to. Category: contestant-female.
- Roxy Tam — the designated villain, a role she accepted knowingly in
  the audition room. Vicious on camera, warm and self-aware off it.
  Category: contestant-female.
- Deshawn Price — kind, funny, hopeless; entered as a joke by his
  sister. Safest person to trust, most dangerous person to be caught
  betraying. Category: contestant-male.
- Trev Grubb — the only returning player, out fourth on an earlier
  season. Understands the machinery better than anyone except Priya and
  will teach the player how the show really works. Loathes Dorian.
  Category: contestant-male.

Gender split across the roster: 8 female, 7 male.

THE TWINS
------------
Vela and Nova Quinn share an identical "identity" line except for one
deliberate tell: Vela wears her hair in a high ponytail, Nova wears hers
loose. Same face, same build, same colouring — so their Stage A
portraits read as genuinely identical twins — with a single reliable
visual difference the player can learn, and which the twins swap on
purpose when it suits them.

Identical portraits via a shared locked seed is NOT possible at
authoring time: baseline seeds are assigned as
Math.floor(Math.random() * 999999) per NPC on first encounter
(generateNpcBaseline()), not derived from the NPC's name, so there is no
story-definition field that could pin two NPCs to the same seed. The
matching identity text achieves the same effect without engine changes,
and the one-detail tell is better for play than true indistinguishability.

CAST WEIGHTING
-----------------
Fifteen NPCs is above Solmere's twelve, and the prompt builder already
scopes off-screen NPCs down to a single name line, so context size is
not the constraint — flattening is. promptRules.setting therefore states
an explicit weighting, since the engine has no per-NPC tier field:
- Main storylines: Dorian, Vela, Nova, Cody, Ingrid, Roxy, Renata
- Strong supporting: Deshawn, Trev, Josefa, Bastien, Camille, Priya
- Background: Teo, Milo
with an instruction not to give all fifteen equal time in a scene.

STANDARD UNIFORMS (Stage A baseline portraits)
---------------------------------------------------
Six categories, each combined with an NPC's identity line and a generic
neutral background at generation time — not written per-NPC, so changing
one line updates every NPC sharing that Category. A matching
poolUniforms set covers the three wet locations.
- contestant-male / contestant-female: branded high-vis work kit, tool
  belt, work boots, dust and sweat, cut short and tight.
- staff-male / staff-female: Halcyon resort uniform, faded palm crest,
  crisp but undone in the tropical heat.
- production-male / production-female: pale linen, lanyard and radio,
  sunglasses pushed into the hair, camera-ready.

MYSTERY SITE
---------------
The Sunken Villa — the resort's flooded west end, fenced, excluded from
the renovation, and barred from filming by the show's contract with the
owners. Renata holds the only key and refuses to discuss it. Teo was
there in 2014. Josefa knows about half. Dorian wants it on camera by the
finale. Season-long slow burn, revealed in pieces and only to a player
who earns the staff's trust. Fills the same structural role as Solmere's
Restricted Wing and Meridian's Cargo Bay.

ADVENTURE SETUP FIELDS (Halcyon-specific)
----------------------------------------------
- Your Trade — Carpenter / Electrician / Plumber / Tiler / Landscaper
  (flavours the player's expertise; does not change uniform category)
- Your Edit — The Hero / The Villain / The Wildcard / The Underdog /
  The Flirt (how production has cast the player; steers narration tone
  and how NPCs initially read them)
- Plus: Character Name, Gender, Appearance, Age (required, universal —
  see Features.txt ADVENTURE SETUP SCREEN). No freeform custom fields —
  curated fields only, per Adventure.

playerCategoryTemplate: contestant-{playerGender}. The player is always
a contestant, so unlike Solmere's {socialStanding}-student-{playerGender}
the template has a single variable.
