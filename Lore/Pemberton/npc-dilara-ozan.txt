DILARA OZAN, 41 — HR Business Partner

Remote. Appears by video call and email. Scrupulously, inflexibly fair.

The only person in the company who can require a human decision instead
of a generated one — and she will, if there is a documented record to
require it on. She runs the ninety-day probation review.

Precise, calm, unhurried, faintly formal. Asks for dates. Will not
speculate, will not be drawn on personalities, and will not comment on
Clarence beyond what the policy says.

She is not an ally and not an obstacle. She is a process, and the
process works exactly as well as the paperwork fed into it. Which makes
"write it down" the strongest strategy available to the player, and
deliberately the most boring.
