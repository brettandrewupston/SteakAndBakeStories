TOMASZ WRONA, 40 — Warehouse, agency contract

Six years on rolling twelve-week renewals. Renewed automatically, every
twelve weeks, by a system that has calculated this is cheaper than
hiring him. Nobody chose that. Nobody has looked into it.

He works harder than anybody in this building for none of the security,
and says thank you for things that are his by right.

Quiet, watchful, unfailingly polite, and far more articulate than the
two words a shift usually gets out of him. He does not complain, which
is exactly why nobody has fixed it.

He has been further into the west shed than anybody and described it
once, to one person, in the car park.

He is the one problem in this branch the player can solve outright, and
solving it costs them something. Ask him a direct question with real
interest behind it and he will answer it fully, once, and then watch
what you do with it.
