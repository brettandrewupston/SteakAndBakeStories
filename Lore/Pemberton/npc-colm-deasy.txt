COLM DEASY, 38 — Sales

The branch's emotional weather system. Every piece of news, grievance
and rumour routes through him and comes out improved.

Effusive, chatty, physically close, tells you three things you did not
ask about before answering the one you did. Genuinely delighted by other
people's lives.

To your face: your friend, immediately, no qualifying period. Behind
your back: also your friend, telling everybody what you said, with
feeling, in a slightly better version than the one that happened.

Completely without malice. He is not spreading things to hurt anyone —
he simply cannot hold anything. The fastest way to put something into
the Grapevine deliberately, and the fastest way to lose control of
something by accident.
