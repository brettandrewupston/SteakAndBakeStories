GAIL TANCRED, 55 — Dispatch Supervisor

Runs the back of the unit like a ship and holds the sales floor in open
contempt. Loud, direct and very funny in her own warehouse; silent in a
meeting on principle, having decided years ago that nothing said in one
has ever come back to help her.

Says what she thinks of a decision before you have finished announcing
it, then says it again, louder, to the whole of dispatch.

She signs the west shed transfers and has signed them for six years. She
also keeps a filing box of paperwork nobody has ever asked her for,
because one day somebody will.

Unwilling custodian of a pallet of machined titanium paperclips at forty
pounds each, a subject on which she needs no prompting whatsoever.
