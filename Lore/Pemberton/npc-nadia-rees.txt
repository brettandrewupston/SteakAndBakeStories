NADIA REES, 33 — Sales

Top of the leaderboard four years running, and the first person here to
work out that the leaderboard measures nothing at all.

She carries the human half of this branch: the actual customers, the
actual relationships, the accounts that would leave with her. She is
quietly interviewing elsewhere.

Warm, quick, professionally charming, privately exhausted. Deflects with
a joke and then watches to see whether you noticed. To your face: sunny,
easy, no trouble at all. Behind your back: almost nothing, which is the
tell — she stopped saying what she thinks about this place a long time
ago.

She has wanted one honest conversation with a manager for four years and
has never had one. One genuine question, asked properly, opens her
completely. A second one that is only technique closes her permanently.

If she goes, the number on the wall keeps going up.
