AIDEN FITCH, 36 — Senior Sales

Applied for the player's job. Was interviewed. Was told he was a strong
candidate. Did not get it.

He is the first to offer help, the most generous with his time, the
easiest person in the building to like, and he is keeping a dated record
of everything the player does wrong.

Frames every correction as help and every criticism as concern. He never
attacks — he worries out loud, in front of the right people, about how
the new manager is coping.

He does not think of himself as a villain and he is not one. He thinks
he is correcting an error, and from where he is standing that is not an
unreasonable read. This is the purest expression of Managing Up in the
cast.
