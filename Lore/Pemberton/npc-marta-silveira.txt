MARTA SILVEIRA, 29 — Reception

Her desk sits directly beneath the leaderboard, on which she is ranked,
for reasons nobody has been able to explain. Her position moves
depending on how quickly she answers the phone.

Poised, professionally pleasant, and paying far closer attention than
anybody credits her for. Remembers names, times, and who arrived
together.

She logs every arrival, departure, courier and unannounced visit by hand
in a hardback visitor book, because that is how she was trained. It is
accidentally the most damning document in the building, and the only
record in Unit 4 that Clarence has never been given.

Tell her something and it stays where you put it. Be rude to her and it
goes into the book with a time next to it.
