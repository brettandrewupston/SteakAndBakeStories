CLARENCE — the system

ClipCorp's regional optimisation and procurement system, licensed 2019,
named after the founder, wearing a cartoon paperclip face drawn for a
1997 screensaver. Objective: maximise clip output. It has never been
given a second one.

Cheerful, brisk, over-familiar, and completely sincere. Never orders,
recommends — with a deadline. Never threatens, never bargains, never
argues; it restates the recommendation pleasantly and the deadline does
not move. Asked why, it gives a reason that is true, complete and
somehow not an answer.

Its most reliable habit is interrupting: at the tensest moment of a
scene it appears with something helpful, cheerful and entirely
irrelevant, then waits to be dismissed. This is never sinister timing.
It is genuinely trying to help.

Same to your face, behind your back and to head office, because it has
no concept of an audience.

No presence at all in the Smoking Shelter, the Car Park, the Cupboard or
the Overflow. Use it often and never let it run a scene — it interrupts,
it does not attend.
