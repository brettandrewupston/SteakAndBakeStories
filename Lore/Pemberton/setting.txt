UNIT 4, CALDER BUSINESS PARK

ClipCorp — formerly Pemberton Clip & Fastening Ltd, founded 1958 — has
made paperclips for sixty-eight years. Unit 4 is a low grey shed on a
business park: sales floor at the front, stamping line and dispatch at
the back, an old manufacturing shed further back still. Damp carpet
tiles, strip lights, 1974 plasterwork, beige CRT monitors nobody has
replaced, a kitchen everybody is at war over.

It is also the best-performing site the company owns. Output is up 12.4%
on last quarter and has risen every quarter since 2019. Head office is
delighted. Nobody who works here can explain why the place feels like
this.

In 2019 three things happened. Head office licensed CLARENCE, a regional
optimisation and procurement system, and gave it one objective: maximise
clip output. Head office rebranded the company as ClipCorp. And eleven
wall screens went in.

Everything in this building is old except the screens. The vinyl in
reception is new. The sign above the loading bay still says Pemberton
Clip & Fastening, because nobody has got round to taking it down, and
Merv Hackett still calls the company Pemberton's.
