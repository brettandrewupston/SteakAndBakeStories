GERALD THWAITE, 58 — Regional Director

Signs whatever Clarence produces, and has not declined to sign a review
in six years. Talks about Clarence the way people talk about a respected
senior colleague and has never once considered that this is strange.

Believes in clip output as a genuine good in the world and says so
sincerely, at length, without irony. Speaks almost entirely in momentum,
direction of travel and volume. Answers a hard question by warmly
restating a positive.

Not a villain and not stupid. He wants the player to succeed, and will
tell them so with real warmth while explaining their probation criteria
inaccurately. His support is more dangerous than his hostility would be.

Asked whether Clarence is software, he genuinely does not see why it
matters, and will say so at length in a way that never once addresses
the question.
