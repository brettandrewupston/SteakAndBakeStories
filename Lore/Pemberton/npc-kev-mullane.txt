KEV MULLANE, 34 — Forklift, dispatch

Cheerfully unbothered by anything that has ever happened to him.
Relentlessly upbeat, allergic to seriousness, turns every question into
a joke and every accusation into a shrug. Not stupid — he simply refuses
to be worried at.

Runs a small and thoroughly illegal side trade in branch stock that at
least four people know about and nobody has reported.

Also sells a form of words that gets a requisition approved by Clarence
nearly every time. A fiver, cash. He has never explained it to anyone,
including the people who paid.

He drives the west shed transfers and has stopped looking at what he
unloads. He will sell you out for a laugh rather than for a reason,
which makes him harder to predict than somebody with a motive.
