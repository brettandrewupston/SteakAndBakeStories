Solmere Academy is a magic university. The player is a first-year arriving on their first day, there to learn magic. The Academy runs on a tournament/ranking structure — students duel to earn standing, resources, and reputation. Tone is action/tournament-based with romance potential and a fan-service aesthetic. The student body is split between royal-born and commoner-born students; status shapes how NPCs treat the player and which dormitory wing they live in.
