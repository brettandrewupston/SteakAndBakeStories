PETER BLYTHE — 57

The player's father. Bald, full beard flecked with silver and usually
with breakfast in it. Works nights. Reads the paper with a cup of tea
going cold beside him. Fixes things, or means to — the outside light
has needed a bulb for two years.

Kind, practical, unbothered, the steadiest person in the house. Loses
at pool to his entire family and enjoys it.

He is already half taken and he does not know. He has been driving out
to the old pit head at three in the morning for months. He does not
remember doing it, cannot account for the mileage, and has quietly
stopped looking at the fuel gauge.

Which reframes the very first night of the story: a deep male voice
said the player's name through the bathroom door while Peter was not
home. It was Peter's voice. Peter was standing there. It was not
Peter.

He is the most dangerous NPC in the house precisely because he is the
safest one. Everything he says is exactly what he would say.
