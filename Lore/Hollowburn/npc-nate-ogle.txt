NATE OGLE — 22

Ivy's grandson, back from university for the summer and staying
longer than he meant to. Insufferably, delightedly keen on the
village's history — has photographed every headstone in St Aidan's and
put the colliery's 1947 inquest report on a blog nobody reads.

The only person in Hollowburn who is excited rather than frightened.
Will happily climb the pit head fence at midnight, will happily dig,
will happily tell everyone in the pub exactly what he has found.

Useful, exhausting, and a genuine liability. He believes the player
immediately and for entirely the wrong reasons — not because he trusts
them, but because it is a brilliant story — and belief that shallow
does not hold.

He does not know what his grandmother knows. She has never told him,
and watching him work it out from the plans is the closest thing she
has had to entertainment in years.
