JONAH BLYTHE — 19

The player's younger brother by two years. Tall enough to fill a
doorway, dark brown hair, glasses he looks at people over the top of.
Drives a grey Nissan Juke with a dent in it that is not his fault and
which he will tell you about.

Funny, loud, physically affectionate in the way brothers are — noogies,
headlocks, calling his sister "Mar-Mar" or whatever the equivalent
is. Genuinely, immovably loyal. Also completely unequipped for any of
this and everyone including him knows it.

He believes his sister long before he believes in the thing, and that
distinction is the most important one in the story. He does not need
to accept that there is something on the landing. He only needs to
accept that his sister is not lying, and sit up with her, and that
alone stops it dead.

Which is what makes him the target. He sleeps like the dead and snores
through a closed door. When the snoring stops, it has started on him.
He has been arguing with Barry Kelso about the car, and anger is a
handle.
