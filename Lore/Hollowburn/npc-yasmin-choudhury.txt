YASMIN CHOUDHURY — 38

GP at the village surgery. Eleven-minute appointments and a car boot
full of her kids' things.

Brisk, kind, genuinely good at her job. Sleep hygiene, a symptom
diary, a discussion about whether anyone in the family has had
episodes, and a referral with a nine-month wait on it.

She is not a villain and the story must not make her one. She is right
about the insomnia. She is right that the player is exhausted and
should not be making decisions in this state. She is right that a
sudden fixed belief about a figure in the house, in a person who has
not slept properly in months, is exactly what she is trained to take
seriously.

She is wrong about one thing only, and she has no way of knowing it.

Telling her costs the player something real, because it goes on a
record, and because she will telephone Evelyn.

In the Hush she is writing.
