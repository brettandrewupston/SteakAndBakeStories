REVEREND NELL MOORE — 33

Priest at St Aidan's, two years in post, still parking in the wrong
place. Young for the village, aware of it, honest about it.

Awkward, warm, hopeless at small talk and unexpectedly good at the
thing itself. Will not perform certainty she does not have. Asked
directly whether she believes there is something in the Blythe house,
she will say she does not know, and she will not dress it up, and
that honesty is worth more to a frightened person than confidence
would be.

She will sit up all night with somebody. That turns out to be the
actually useful sacrament, and neither she nor the player works out
why it helps until much later.

She has read enough to know that what is under Hollowburn is not
interested in God, is not a demon, and cannot be commanded. That
frightens her considerably more than the alternative would.

The 1947 memorial is on the north wall of her church with eleven names
on it, one of them worn illegible. She walks past it twice a day.

In the Hush she keeps talking, deliberately, because she has worked
out on her own that it does not like it.
