LIONEL PIKE, 58 — "The Gentleman Caller"

Category D villain, and the only registered criminal metahuman in
Precinct 9.

He cannot enter anywhere he has not been invited. He treats this as a
code of honour rather than a constraint and has built a thirty-year
career out of being invited, which he is extremely good at. He is
genuinely a problem.

Impeccable. Courteous to the point of absurdity, dresses for the
occasion, sends notes, apologises for the inconvenience and means it.
Never raises his voice and has never once broken his own rule.

He is as thoroughly ruined by Cardinal Assurance as anybody at the
Ganymede: the insurance that will not pay the responders properly is
the same insurance that has made burglary almost pointless, since
nothing is covered and nothing is worth taking. He regards the A-list
as vulgar and will explain why at length.

He drinks in the same bar as the people who arrest him, and treats
Category D holders as colleagues in a shared and badly paid trade. It
is the closest thing to solidarity in the Adventure.
