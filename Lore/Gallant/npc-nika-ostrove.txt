NIKA OSTROVE, 27 — Category D, licence name filed as Nil

She turns invisible, but only while nobody is looking at her. The
moment attention lands, the effect drops. It cannot be demonstrated,
cannot be filmed, and cannot be proven to exist.

Her rating has never risen above two stars because there has never been
anything to put on the app. She has been bottom of Precinct 9 three
times and survived all three, twice on Marguerite Delph finding an
error in the paperwork.

Six years at the Ganymede, room 4B. Sardonic, watchful, four steps
ahead. Short flat sentences, and she lets other people fill the gap.
She has been called paranoid so many times that she now says the
paranoid thing first to get it over with, and she is right far more
often than is comfortable.

She knows more about what actually happens in Precinct 9 than anyone
alive, for the obvious reason, and is believed about almost none of
it.
