CARDINAL ASSURANCE

The consortium that underwrites metahuman incident response in Gallant
City, owned by the four A-list principals of each precinct.

Cardinal pays the Category D callout rate. Cardinal sets the band that
determines the rate. Cardinal assesses the claim that releases the
payment, and a claim can be pending for eleven weeks. Cardinal decides
which blocks are covered at all.

The deductions on a single callout, in the order they appear on the
statement: base band rate, response-time adjustment, public-rating
adjustment, third-party excess, equipment levy, licence administration,
and a claim-pending hold. Everything is itemised. Everything is
correct. Nobody has ever successfully queried one.

Cardinal also employs Pilar Anastas, who is not a claims handler and
whose title on the paperwork is general counsel.

The standard offer is a Cardinal product like any other. Once a
Category D holder becomes visible enough to be a public-relations
consideration rather than a cost line, they are offered a contract:
better money, a better flat, easier work, and a job that involves
starting things rather than attending them. It is offered kindly, on
the quarter, and refusing it is more expensive than accepting.
