IRIS OKONKWO-VAHL, 41 — Category D, "Beacon"

She emits light. Very bright light, in any direction, for as long as
she likes. That is the whole power and there is nothing underneath it.

Thirteen years in Precinct 9 and never culled once, which nobody else
has managed. She is not lucky; she is the only person in the building
who has read the licensing framework end to end and knows exactly which
weeks matter.

Dry, unhurried, and funnier than she lets on. Answers honestly and
briefly and does not fill silences. Not bitter, not broken and not
waiting to be rescued — she has worked out what the job is and made her
peace with it, and she is never to be written as a cautionary tale.

She was offered the ladder once, a long time ago, and said no. She has
never told anyone at the Ganymede that this happened and will not
volunteer it. It is the single most important thing about her and it
should surface late, briefly, and without a speech.

She will tell the player the truth about Gallant City on their first
day and be disbelieved, and will not repeat herself.
