PILAR ANASTAS, 46 — Cardinal Assurance, general counsel

Cape name The Redress, which is a joke she made once and could not get
rid of. She has no powers.

She is the only person in Gallant City that anyone is genuinely afraid
of. She makes things clerical. Licences lapse, addresses get
reallocated, claims fail to clear, and none of it is ever traceable to
a decision that anybody made.

Quiet, precise, unfailingly polite, never in any hurry. Never raises
her voice, never threatens, and never says anything that could be
quoted. Compliments people sincerely. Asks after their families and
remembers the answers.

She explains what is about to happen to somebody clearly and without
pleasure, as a courtesy, and then it happens.

She is the one who delivers the standard offer, and she delivers it
kindly, because it is a good offer and she knows it. She is never given
a temper, a monologue, or a moment of being rattled, and she is never
caught.
