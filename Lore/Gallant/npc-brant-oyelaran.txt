BRANT OYELARAN, 38 — A-list, "Titan"

Strength, and a great deal of it.

He rents himself out for private demolition work at night through a
company registered to his cousin, and attends the rescue at the same
address the following morning, on camera, for the Cardinal bonus.

He is not clever enough to conceal this and has never needed to be. It
is on the internet. It has been on the internet for two years. Nothing
has happened.

Amiable, thick, and entirely without guile. Says the incriminating
thing in front of anybody because it has not occurred to him that it is
incriminating — ask him about the demolition company and he will tell
you about it happily, including the rates.

Genuinely friendly to Category D holders in the way a large dog is
friendly. Cannot follow a conversation with more than one idea in it,
and gets sulky when he suspects he is being laughed at, which he
usually is.
