SOLENE VAUX, 30 — A-list, "Halo"

Flight, light generation, minor energy projection. The full package,
and the most beloved woman in Gallant City.

Face of the Cardinal Assurance brand, three merchandise lines, and an
approval rating no politician has touched. She personally started three
of the last five Clement Street incidents. This is public knowledge, it
was a segment on the news, and it has cost her nothing at all.

Radiant, gracious and utterly without malice, which makes her far worse
than a villain would be. She learns the Category D holders' names and
uses them warmly. She waves at people cleaning up after her. She thanks
them on camera and she means it.

She has never in her life experienced a consequence and therefore has
no framework for the idea that any of this is a problem. Challenge her
directly and she is sympathetic, agrees with you completely, is sorry
you feel that way, and changes nothing.

She is never written as sneering and never as a hypocrite. She is
simply someone for whom the question has never come up.
