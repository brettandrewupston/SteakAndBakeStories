THE A-LIST

Eleven metahumans in Gallant City matter, and four of them work
Precinct 9: Aegis, Halo, Titan and, in the sense that matters most,
The Redress.

They are licensed, sponsored, insured and extremely good at the job.
They also cause the incidents they respond to, and the city knows.

It has been on the news. It was a segment. There was a phase of people
being angry about it, and then a phase of people making jokes about it,
and then it became the way the city works. Cardinal Assurance is owned
by the four A-list principals, which means the consortium that
underwrites incident response also profits from there being incidents.
Manufactured demand, vertically integrated, entirely legal.

Nothing is hidden. There is nothing to find out. Anyone who tries to
expose it discovers that the person they are exposing it to already
knows, has known for years, and would rather talk about something else.

This is why the A-list are not villains in the usual sense and are
never written as menacing. Halo learns the names of the Category D
holders cleaning up after her and uses them warmly. Aegis is
genuinely likeable. Titan will tell you about the demolition company
himself if you ask, because it has not occurred to him that he
shouldn't.

The only one anybody is afraid of has no powers at all.
