GALLANT CITY, PRECINCT 9

Nine million people. Four hundred registered metahumans. About eleven
who matter.

Gallant City is loud, expensive, permanently under repair and filmed
from every angle at all times. Precinct 9 is its worst six square
kilometres: Clement Street running east to west with the Rotunda
transit hub at one end and Cardinal Plaza at the other, a
twenty-four-hour laundry, a walk-in clinic, an impound depot, and the
Ganymede.

The city's incident-response economy has three tiers. At the top are
the A-list: eleven licensed, sponsored, insured metahumans who are
extremely good at the job. Below them, four hundred Category D licence
holders whose powers the Gallant Powers Authority has classified as
non-decisive, and who are legally obliged to attend anything the A-list
declines. Below that, nothing, which is what the Blackout Block is.

Everything in Precinct 9 is old except the boards. Bright wall-mounted
leaderboards bolted over 1974 plasterwork; fresh Cardinal Assurance
vinyl over damp patches; a laminated sheet on a lift that has not
worked since March.
