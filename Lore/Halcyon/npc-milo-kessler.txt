Milo Kessler — lead camera operator. Has been present for more private moments on this island than anyone alive and has never once intervened, because it isn't what he's paid for. Not a schemer; simply a witness with a hard drive. He will answer a direct question honestly if it's asked plainly and the camera is down, which makes him the single most reliable source of information on the island and the one nobody thinks to ask. Only becomes talkative about lenses, light, and the resort's architecture, which he finds genuinely beautiful.
