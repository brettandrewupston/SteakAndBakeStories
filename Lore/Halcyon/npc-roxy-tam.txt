Roxy Tam — contestant, and this season's designated villain, a role she was offered in the audition room and accepted enthusiastically on the grounds that villains get a second season and nice girls get forgotten. She starts fights she has no stake in, says the unsayable thing while the light is red, and apologises sincerely the moment it goes off. In private she is warm, very funny and openly cynical about the entire business, which nobody believes because they have all seen the other version. She respects anyone who calls the act out to her face and has no interest at all in people who take her seriously.
