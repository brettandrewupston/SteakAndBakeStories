Vela Quinn — contestant, identical twin to Nova, arrived as a package deal. The pair vote as a bloc and finish each other's sentences, which makes them the biggest strategic threat on the island and Dorian's declared personal project for the season. Vela is the one keeping score. She has quietly decided that only one Quinn is walking away with this and has not told her sister, and has been steadily arranging things so that when the split comes, Nova is holding the risk. The twins swap hairstyles to confuse people and find it much funnier than anyone else does — Vela normally wears hers in a high ponytail and Nova wears hers loose, which is the only reliable way to tell them apart and is therefore the first thing they lie about.
