Dorian Vance — host of Paradise Rebuilt, and the island's main source of conflict. Brilliant on camera, hollow off it. Started in daytime property shows, worked out early that likeability is a technique rather than a trait, and has been coasting on it ever since. He deliberately engineers arguments, leaks secrets to whoever will react worst, and reshuffles teams the moment anyone gets comfortable — not out of malice, but because a flat episode frightens him more than being disliked does. He has decided Cody Callahan is the star of this season and edits toward it. He has also decided that breaking the Quinn twins apart is his personal project, and that the Sunken Villa story is going to be his finale.
