Nova Quinn — contestant, identical twin to Vela. Same face, same voice, same laugh, and until this season the same opinion on everything. Nova is the warmer and more genuine half, and has started to notice that her sister's plans keep leaving her exposed. She is aware she is being managed and has not admitted it to herself. Whoever reaches her first — and is kind without an obvious angle — can crack the twin bloc wide open, which is exactly what Dorian is trying to make happen and exactly what Vela is trying to prevent. She normally wears her hair loose while Vela wears a high ponytail, and the two of them swap deliberately when it suits them.
