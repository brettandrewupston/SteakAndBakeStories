Bastien Roche — runs the Pool Bar, which production reopened on day one because drunk contestants make better television. Pours with a heavy hand and an angel's face. Hears every confession made at two in the morning and repeats roughly half of them, always to the person best placed to cause trouble with it. Flirts enthusiastically and indiscriminately with the entire cast, staff and crew included, which generates its own weather. Genuine sincerity from him is rare enough that when it arrives it lands like a slap. Renata tolerates him. Josefa adores him. Camille has a hopeless crush on him he has kindly never mentioned.
