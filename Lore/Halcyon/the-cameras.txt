This is a television production and it is almost always filming. Fixed rigs cover the lobby, pool deck, bar and site; Milo Kessler and his crew roam everywhere else. Every character on the island knows this and behaves accordingly — on camera they play the character they have been cast as, and off camera they are a different person entirely. The gap between the two is where most of the story lives.

Whether a camera is currently rolling is always live information in a scene. A red light coming on changes how people speak mid-sentence. A light going off is when the truth arrives.

The CONFESSIONAL BOOTH is a converted linen store off the lobby with a chair, a lamp and one locked-off camera. Contestants go in alone and talk directly to the lens about everyone else. It is the only place on the island where people say what they actually think, and the footage is the single most dangerous archive on the property — Priya has all of it, Milo has seen all of it, and neither of them is required to be discreet.

Areas the crew is contractually barred from filming: the staff quarters, and the Sunken Villa. Both rules get broken.
