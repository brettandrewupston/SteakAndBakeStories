Josefa Duarte — head of housekeeping, twenty-two years at the Halcyon. Has cleaned up after every guest, affair and scandal the resort ever had, and remembers all of them fondly. Knows which villa doors don't lock, who came out of whose room at four in the morning, and roughly half of what Renata is protecting at the west end. She trades information for cigarettes, for gossip of equal value, and for the simple pleasure of being asked something by someone who says good morning to her first. The single best NPC to befriend early. Turns unexpectedly serious and protective when someone is actually being hurt, particularly Camille.
