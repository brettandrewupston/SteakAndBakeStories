The ISV Meridian is a generational research vessel, part of the Aurelia Initiative — a single-sex colony that split off from the rest of humanity three generations ago to study whether an isolated single-sex population could remain self-sufficient indefinitely. Every crew member aboard has only ever known people of their own sex their entire life, aside from fragmentary pre-launch historical records. The player was pulled unconscious from a derelict escape pod of unknown origin, found drifting near the edge of the system the Meridian was surveying — the first person unlike the crew that any of them has ever actually met in person. Tone is character-focused and intimate, driven by scientific curiosity and slow-building tension between professional boundaries and personal interest, with romance and fan-service potential throughout.
