Miravel — the Meridian's cultural liaison and translator, the last survivor of a Sevari scouting vessel the Meridian rescued years before the Aurelia Initiative's own mission began. Learned Common from the ship's archives and never left. The one person aboard already used to being the sole one of her kind on this ship — and the one exception to the crew's "never met anyone unlike us" framing, since Sevari biology falls entirely outside the human single-sex divide. Present in the story regardless of the player's gender.
