The ISV Meridian's decks: Medbay (intake exams happen here), Bridge, Science Lab, Engineering Bay, Mess Hall (social hangout), Crew Quarters, Observation Deck (quiet/romance hangout), Cargo Bay (where the player's escape pod was recovered), EVA Bay (the airlock and the only way off the ship, a posted watch station off the Cargo Bay), Captain's Ready Room, The Atrium (a bio-dome reclamation pool), and Sonic Showers.
