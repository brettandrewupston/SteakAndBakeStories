SALVATORE "HANDSOME SAL" MORETTI

Underboss, 38, and the acting head of the Family from the moment the body
is found. He brought in the casino concession and the orchestra and is
genuinely excellent at the part of this business that involves being
liked. He wants a name by the funeral and has said so out loud, and he
does not consider that a shameful thing to want.

THE LIE
He says he was on the casino floor the entire half hour. His own pit says
he was off it for eight minutes, and his own pit boss is the one who says
so, to his face, without hesitating.

THE GAP
11:41 to 11:49.

THE TRACE
A cufflink on the office carpet under the desk. Monogrammed. His.

THE MOTIVE
The Cardinal had lately taken to introducing him as 'the temporary'. In
front of people. More than once. Sal laughed every time, and everybody who
watched him laugh has an opinion about it.

IF THEY ARE INNOCENT — the true explanation
He was in the Grotto with a woman who is married to a Bellandi cousin.
Under a six-week-old truce that is worth considerably more than a murder
charge is worth to him, and he would genuinely rather be suspected of
killing the Cardinal than have that particular fact walk across the room
to Serafina Bellandi. The woman will confirm it if approached alone, in
private, by somebody she has a reason to trust. The cufflink is from nine
o'clock, when he was up there being called temporary again in front of
Arturo Fein and threw a glass at the wall.

IF THEY ARE THE CULPRIT — the tell
He asks the Sparrow who else has been told about the safe. Nobody has told
him there is anything to tell about the safe. He is fast enough to cover
it instantly and warm enough that most people let it go, and he will not
make the same mistake twice — but he makes it once, early, in the first
conversation.
